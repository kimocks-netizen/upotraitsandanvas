Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: June 2025

We deliver your finished canvas to your door before collecting full payment. You inspect it first — you only pay when you are satisfied.

1. Delivery Areas

  • We currently deliver within South Africa.
  • Free delivery applies to orders within our standard local delivery zones. Contact us to confirm coverage for your area before placing your order.
  • Delivery to areas outside our standard zones may incur an additional fee, which will be communicated to you before dispatch.

2. Production Time

  • All canvas portraits are custom-made to order. Production typically takes 3–7 business days after your order is confirmed and your photograph has been approved.
  • We source some materials from suppliers. In rare cases, material availability may affect production time. We will notify you promptly if a delay is expected.

3. Delivery Time

  • Once your canvas is ready, delivery is arranged via third-party courier services.
  • Estimated delivery time after dispatch is 2–5 business days depending on your location.
  • You will be contacted to confirm a suitable delivery time before dispatch.

4. Packaging

  • All orders are carefully packaged to protect your canvas during transit.
  • Packaging is provided at no additional cost.

5. Delivery & Payment

  • Our delivery model is unique: your canvas is delivered before full payment is collected.
  • On delivery, you open and inspect your canvas. If you are satisfied, you pay the remaining balance. If you are not satisfied, we take the canvas back at no charge to you.
  • The courier or delivery agent will confirm payment collection at the point of delivery.

6. Damaged or Lost in Transit

  • We package all orders carefully to minimise the risk of damage.
  • If your order arrives visibly damaged, please photograph the packaging and product immediately and contact us within 48 hours of delivery at info@vula-pay.site.
  • We will assess the situation and arrange a replacement or refund where applicable.
  • We are not liable for loss or damage caused by circumstances beyond our control once the order has been handed to the courier.
