# 13. Important Checkout Change

## Remove the Current R10 Upfront Deposit

The new Vula-Pay proposition is:

> **No upfront payment. Open first. Pay when satisfied.**

Therefore the current **R10 Paystack confirmation deposit** conflicts with the main promise and should be removed if the business is committing to the Vula-Pay model.

### Recommended Order Flow

1. Customer adds canvas to order.
2. Customer enters their contact and delivery information.
3. Customer accepts the terms.
4. Customer clicks **Place My Order — No Payment Now**.
5. Order is saved/sent to your business.
6. Your team confirms the photograph and specification.
7. Canvas is made.
8. Canvas is delivered.
9. Customer opens and inspects it.
10. If satisfied, customer completes payment.
11. If not satisfied or they have changed their mind, the product is taken back at zero cost to them.

### Paystack
Paystack can still be used for the **final payment**, but the payment should occur **after the customer accepts the delivered product**, not when the order is initially placed.

# 19. FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to pay before you make my canvas?
**No.** Under the Vula-Pay model, you place your order without paying the full amount upfront.

### When do I pay?
When your canvas is delivered, you open and inspect it first. **You pay only when you are satisfied with the finished product.**

### What happens if I do not like it?
If you are not satisfied at delivery or you have changed your mind, **we take the product back at zero cost to you.**

### Is delivery free?
**Yes, where your Vula-Pay delivery offer applies.** Make sure the website clearly states the actual delivery coverage before launch.

### Can I use my own photograph?
Yes. Your own photograph can be turned into a personalised canvas.

### Can you help me choose a size?
Yes. If you are unsure, contact us and we can help you choose a size that suits your wall and image.

### Can I request a custom size?
Yes. Custom sizes can be quoted based on your requirements.

### Can I choose the canvas edge?
Yes. Available options can include full wrap, half/mirrored wrap, solid colour and white wrap.

### What if my photograph is not suitable?
Your team should review the image before production. If a photograph is unlikely to produce a good canvas, contact the customer before making the order and request a better image where possible.

### Do you offer photography?
Photography can be offered at an additional cost where the service is available.

### How long does my order take?
Add your actual production and delivery times here once confirmed. Do not promise a delivery time that cannot consistently be met.

### How do I pay after delivery?
List the actual methods you will accept at delivery, such as a secure card/payment link, EFT or another supported method.
