Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering with Vula&Pay.

Do I have to pay before you make my canvas?

No. Under the Vula&Pay model, you place your order without paying the full amount upfront.

When do I pay?

When your canvas is delivered, you open and inspect it first. You pay only when you are satisfied with the finished product.

What happens if I do not like it?

If you are not satisfied at delivery or you have changed your mind, we take the product back at zero cost to you.

Is delivery free?

Yes, free delivery on all orders above 500 Rands or in Delivery Zones. Contact us to confirm delivery coverage for your area.

Can I use my own photograph?

Yes. Your own photograph can be turned into a personalised canvas portrait.

Can I request a custom size?

Yes. Custom sizes can be quoted based on your requirements. Contact us with your wall dimensions.

Can I choose the canvas edge finish?

Yes. Available options include full wrap, half/mirrored wrap, solid colour and white wrap.

What if my photograph is not suitable?

We review your image before production. If a photograph is unlikely to produce a good canvas, we will contact you and request a better image where possible.

Do you offer photography?

Photography can be arranged at an additional cost where the service is available. Contact us to enquire.

Can you help me choose a size?

Yes. Send us a photo of your wall or your approximate dimensions and we will help you choose the right size.
